# MJ-14 Audio loudness baseline Plan

> **For agentic workers:** a research procedure. No change to Montaj's code, defaults, database or masters. Experimental audio copies live only in a scratch folder. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** Choose a peak-safe loudness target for Montaj's clips from measured evidence and a blind listening test on the four pilot clips, and hand MJ-3 a recommendation: target, method, and whether Montaj's current constant-gain policy is enough.

**Spec:** Jira [MJ-14](https://rahmaqanater.atlassian.net/browse/MJ-14). It serves MJ-3. The MJ-2 review table (`docs/pilot/2026-11-mj-2-review.md`, branch `docs/mj-2-pilot-review`) holds the first measurements.

## What we know (2026-11-25)

Measured with FFmpeg 9.1 `ebur128` on the four 720p Drive copies:

| Clip | Integrated | True peak | Loudness range |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sonnet #18 | −32.5 LUFS | −10.9 dBTP | 9.0 LU |
| Sonnet #36 | −29.8 LUFS | −12.4 dBTP | 4.5 LU |
| Haiku #47 | −30.6 LUFS | −13.0 dBTP | 4.3 LU |
| Haiku #48 | −30.2 LUFS | −10.9 dBTP | 5.9 LU |

Montaj's opt-in policy (`loudness.linear_gain_db`) applies one constant gain:
- the gain is the smallest of three limits: the gap to the target, `−2 dBTP − true peak`, and `+12 dB`;
- here the peak limit wins, at +8.9 to +11.0 dB;
- so the clips land at −19.4 to −23.6 LUFS, 5 to 10 LU short of −14.

Reaching −14 LUFS on these clips needs roughly +16 to +18.5 dB. That would put the true peak at +3 to +7 dBTP without a limiter, so it needs peak limiting or compression, which constant gain avoids by design.

## Global Constraints

- **Not allowed:** code changes, default changes, database writes, paid API calls, re-rendering, touching any master, publishing.
- **Copies only.** Experimental copies are made from the 720p copies, audio only re-encoded, picture copied, in their own scratch folder. They never enter `data/`. Results are labelled "measured on 720p copies; masters not verified".
- **The owner's ears decide** between variants. The measurements support that call; they do not replace it.
- **Tools.** FFmpeg's built-in `loudnorm` and `ebur128` only. MJ-3 prefers no new dependency when built-in filters suffice.

## Calls made (the owner can overrule)

- **Four variants per clip:**
  - **A:** original;
  - **B:** Montaj's current policy as coded, target −14, ceiling −2 dBTP, at most +12 dB (constant gain, expected −19.4 to −23.6 LUFS);
  - **C:** two-pass `loudnorm` to −16 LUFS, TP −1.5, LRA 11;
  - **D:** two-pass `loudnorm` to −14 LUFS, TP −1.0, LRA 11.

  C and D are where `loudnorm` must fall back from linear to dynamic mode; the measured JSON records whether it did. −16 and −14 are the two targets the ticket names. −1 dBTP matches `loudness.VERIFY_TRUE_PEAK_DBTP`.
- **Blind listening.** The 16 files get random labels from a key kept apart from the listening sheet. The owner listens on phone speakers and on headphones, at one fixed phone volume, and rates each from 1 to 5 for:
  - loudness;
  - pumping or breathing;
  - harshness or distortion;
  - noise raised by the gain.

  The owner also picks a favourite per clip.
- **Report** in `docs/pilot/2026-11-mj-14-loudness.md`, committed on this branch. The audio files are not committed.

## Review Focus

1. Every variant's output is measured again: integrated LUFS, true peak, LRA and `loudnorm`'s normalization type. A variant over its true-peak ceiling is a finding, not a pass.
2. B is computed with Montaj's own `linear_gain_db`, so the report shows what the shipped code would do, not an approximation.
3. The listening key never sits next to the sheet the owner fills in.
4. The recommendation says plainly whether −14 is reachable without a limiter on clips like these, and what that means for Montaj's "never clip" constant-gain design.
5. Nothing outside the scratch folder changes.

---

### Task 1: Make the variants

- [ ] **Step 1: B gain.** For each clip, compute `loudness.linear_gain_db(I, TP, target=-14.0, ceiling=-2.0, max_gain=12.0)` from the measured values, then apply it with `volume=<gain>dB`. Re-encode the audio as AAC at 192 kbps and stream-copy the video.
- [ ] **Step 2: Two-pass loudnorm for C and D.** Pass 1 runs `loudnorm=I=<t>:TP=<tp>:LRA=11:print_format=json` to measure. Pass 2 feeds the measured values back with `linear=true`. Record the pass-2 JSON, including `normalization_type` (linear or dynamic).
- [ ] **Step 3: Measure every output** with `ebur128=peak=true`: integrated, LRA, true peak. Expected: 12 new files and 16 rows of numbers.

### Task 2: Blind listening sheet

- [ ] **Step 1: Shuffle.** Copy the 16 files to `listen/` under random labels (L01 to L16), and write the label key to a separate `key/` folder.
- [ ] **Step 2: Sheet.** Write a sheet the owner fills in: label, then the four 1 to 5 scores, then a favourite per clip group, grouped by clip so each comparison stays within the same speech.
- [ ] **Step 3: The owner listens** and fills the sheet. This can run in the same sitting as the MJ-2 review of the same four clips.

### Task 3: Report and recommendation

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the report** `docs/pilot/2026-11-mj-14-loudness.md`. It holds:
  - the method;
  - the measurement table for all 16 files;
  - the owner's unblinded scores;
  - what B (the shipped policy) achieves.
- [ ] **Step 2: Recommendation for MJ-3:**
  - the target LUFS;
  - the method: constant gain, or loudnorm with a limiter;
  - whether Montaj's policy needs a limiter stage or a higher gain cap;
  - what must be re-verified on masters.

  Every option is labelled "proposal, needs explicit acceptance".
- [ ] **Step 3: Commit** the report and the plan. With the owner's OK, add a Jira comment on MJ-14 linking the report.
